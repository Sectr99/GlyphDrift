Glyphdrift

A quiet live wallpaper for Windows and Mac

Contour maps, ASCII and minimal motion graphics drifting behind your desktop icons. One small app. No installer, no account, no network.

Download for Windows Download for Mac View source

Windows 10 and 11 · macOS 12 or later, Apple silicon and Intel · about 0.7 MB

Made to stay in the background

Behind your icons

Draws on the desktop itself, under your icons and windows. An optional readability veil keeps icon labels crisp.

Light on resources

Most animations use around 1% of a modern CPU. Pauses itself for fullscreen apps and battery saver (Low Power Mode on a Mac), and says so in the tray or menu bar.

A clock that belongs

Topographic drift can show the time and date in a framed box; the contour lines flow around it.

Tweak it live

Speed, scale, frame rate and theme apply as you drag. The settings window opens by the tray or menu bar icon, so the wallpaper is your preview.

Get started

  1. 1

    Download

    Windows: grab glyphdrift.exe from the latest release. There is nothing to install.

    Mac: grab Glyphdrift-macos.zip, open it and move Glyphdrift to Applications.

  2. 2

    Run it

    The wallpaper starts behind your icons, and an icon appears in the tray or menu bar.

    Windows may warn about an unrecognized app: choose More info, then Run anyway.

    macOS blocks the first launch because the app isn't notarized yet: open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.

  3. 3

    Make it yours

    Windows: left-click the tray icon for settings. Turn on Start with Windows from the right-click menu to keep it.

    Mac: click the menu bar icon for settings and quick options. Turn on Open at Login to keep it.
