Privacy

Last updated October 25, 2026

Glyphdrift is a free, independent project by Sectr99. This notice explains what data the app and this website handle. The short version: the app collects nothing, and the website counts visits anonymously, keeping nothing that identifies you.

The Glyphdrift app

  • The app makes no network connections on its own. It has no telemetry, analytics, crash reporting or accounts.
  • On Windows, Check for updates… in the tray menu asks GitHub for the version number of the latest release, only when you click it. If you then click the notification to update, it downloads the new version from that GitHub release. Like any web request, GitHub sees your IP address; the requests carry nothing else about you or your computer (GitHub's privacy statement).
  • Your settings and a small diagnostic log are stored only on your computer, in %APPDATA%\Glyphdrift on Windows or ~/Library/Application Support/Glyphdrift on a Mac. They never leave your computer. Deleting that folder removes them.
  • If you turn on Start with Windows, the app adds one entry to your user's startup list in the Windows registry. On a Mac, Open at Login adds one file, ~/Library/LaunchAgents/com.sectr99.glyphdrift.plist. Turning the option off removes it.

This website

  • The site uses no cookies, advertising, tracking pixels or third-party fonts.
  • To count visits, the site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, a small script from Cloudflare. It reports the page viewed, the site that linked to it, your browser, operating system and device type, your country and how fast the page loaded, and the site owner sees only totals. Cloudflare states that it doesn't use cookies, local storage or fingerprinting for this, and doesn't follow you across sites. Blocking the script, for example with a content blocker, doesn't affect the site.
  • If you pick a theme, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage so the page remembers it. It is never sent anywhere; clearing this site's data in your browser removes it.
  • The site is hosted on Cloudflare. To deliver pages and protect the site against abuse, Cloudflare processes technical data about each request, such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested. See the Cloudflare privacy policy.
  • Downloads and the source code are hosted on GitHub, where the GitHub privacy statement applies.

Legal basis

Where data protection law such as the EU or UK GDPR applies, the limited request data processed by the hosting provider is handled on the basis of a legitimate interest in delivering a working, secure website (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR), and anonymous visit counting on a legitimate interest in understanding how the site is used. Your theme choice is stored only because you asked for it and stays on your device.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, and to complain to your data protection authority. Because neither the app nor this site stores personal data about you, there is normally nothing to access or delete, but you are welcome to ask.

Children

Glyphdrift is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue on GitHub.
